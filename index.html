Croissant's Quest for Enlightenment

You are Croissant, a brave Shiba Inu on a quest to find the legendary Palo Santo Plant of Enlightenment in Palo Alto!

🎮 Controls

  • WASD / Arrows - Move
  • Spacebar - Bark
  • P - Power-ups
  • E - Talk to people

🎯 Quest

  • Find the hidden maze with the Palo Santo
  • Collect 🥩 Steaks for speed
  • Find 🍲 Kibble to restore energy
  • Talk to people for magical powers
  • Avoid 🐰 Rabbits - you'll chase them!
  • Defeat 🧙 The Evil Wizard in duels!

⚠️ Tips

Energy depletes as you move - find Kibble! Bark to push away the wizard!

🌟 Power-up Hub

Select a power to activate (Press P to close)

💧 Water Crossing!

Croissant is hesitant about the water...

Mash SPACEBAR rapidly to convince him to swim!

Fill the bar before time runs out!
