Croissant's Quest for Enlightenment

You are Croissant, a brave Shiba Inu seeking the legendary Palo Santo Plant of Enlightenment hidden deep within a mysterious cave in Palo Alto!

Controls

  • WASD / Arrows - Move (8 directions)
  • Shift + Move - Sprint (drains energy faster)
  • Space - Jump
  • F - Bark / Lightning (after wizard power)
  • E - Interact (talk, sit, sniff, meditate)
  • K - Kick leaves
  • 1-9 - Use power-ups from HUD
  • ESC - Pause menu

Quest

  • Find the hidden cave entrance to the underground maze
  • Navigate the maze while barking at ghosts (face them!)
  • Escape cave-ins or the maze resets!
  • Collect Steaks for health, Kibble for speed boost
  • Collect Gold Bars scattered across the world
  • Climb the mountain for the legendary sword
  • Defeat 5 wizards to gain lightning powers!
  • Talk to special NPCs across Silicon Valley

Beware

Beware of strangers with beards! They will hunt you down.
Watch out for rabbits, moles, bears, and Zuck!
